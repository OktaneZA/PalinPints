# Palindrome Brewing Co — 77% sign set, placement guide

All models scaled to 77% in plan, depth 38 mm.
Overall sign: **2207 mm wide x 403 mm tall** (~2.21 m x 0.40 m).

## Coordinate system
X = mm from the LEFT edge of the ring emblem (sign left edge), to the piece's left (bounding-box) edge.
Y = mm from the BOTTOM of the sign up to the piece's bottom edge.
Gap = horizontal clearance from the previous letter's right bounding-box edge.
The negative P->A gap is intentional: the A's splayed foot tucks under the P's overhang (the letters do not touch).
Round letters (O, G, C) sit 3-4 mm lower and taller than flat letters - optical overshoot in the typeface. Their Y values below already include this.
