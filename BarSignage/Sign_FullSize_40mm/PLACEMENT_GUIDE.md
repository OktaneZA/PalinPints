# Palindrome Brewing Co — FULL-SIZE sign set (40 mm deep), placement guide

Original plan dimensions, depth reduced 44 mm -> 40 mm.
Overall sign: **2866 mm wide x 523 mm tall** (~2.87 m x 0.52 m).

## Coordinate system
X = mm from the LEFT edge of the ring emblem (sign left edge), to the piece's left bounding-box edge.
Y = mm from the BOTTOM of the sign up to the piece's bottom edge.
Gap = horizontal clearance from the previous letter's right bounding-box edge.
The negative P->A gap is intentional: the A's splayed foot tucks under the P's overhang (letters do not touch).
Round letters (O, G, C) sit 4-5 mm lower and taller - optical overshoot; their Y values include it.
